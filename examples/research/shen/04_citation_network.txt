SECTION D: CLAIM MUTATION TRACKING
============================================================

Original measured claim: selected EQ2/CR3 advancement items conditional on play
time/new level, reported by Shen as “no significant gender-based performance
disparity.”

Hub restatement: Rogstad (2022) moves to the esports scene and says the
perception that women are less skilled or cannot compete at the same level due
to inherent disparities “has proven to be false,” citing Taylor and Shen, then
says differences are caused by experience, cultural assumptions, and conditions
that discourage involvement.

Downstream branches:

1. Dong et al. (2018a, 2018b, 2019): “comparable gaming skills,” a moderate
   construct generalization.
2. Holl et al. (2024): “no gender performance gap,” a minor-to-moderate scope
   simplification that accurately mirrors Shen's own strongest prose.
3. Hébert-Ratté et al. (2025): Shen classified as qualitative/case-study
   evidence about harassment impacts, a major attribution error on a different
   claim.
4. UC Davis (2017): retains “advance at least as fast” and both games; the broad
   headline is tempered by specific body text.

Thus caveats sometimes disappear in one citation step. The mutation is not one
linear chain: several independent downstream branches broaden Shen in different
ways. The two clearly cross-source-checked Field 6-related mutations are Rogstad
and the Dong formulations. Hébert-Ratté is a verified but non-Field-6 MAP GAP.

Attribution classifications:

- MISATTRIBUTED: Dong et al. (2018a, 2018b, 2019) assign general “comparable
  gaming skills” support to Shen; Rogstad assigns an esports/inherent-disparity
  claim to Shen; Hébert-Ratté classifies Shen as qualitative harassment-impact

  relationships are CROSS-SOURCE-CONFIRMED. Severity remains claim-specific.
- ATTRIBUTED ELSEWHERE: EMPTY. No retrieved item clearly assigned the audited

mutation, not proof the whole field uniformly equates proxies and constructs.

The selected network also exports across settings (MMOs → World of Tanks →
esports/casual games), but some papers make that movement explicit and supply
new data. There is no intervention program in Field 6, so intervention ratchet,
metric-only improvement, and “more intervention after failure” are N/A to the
mapped claim. No natural experiment expected by the source claim was identified.

Falsification criteria are generally under-specified in broad prose. A decisive
network-level test would be preregistered, comparable performance outcomes
across games/genres and player stages, with equivalence margins and selection-
aware longitudinal models. Recent task-specific studies update rather than
simply reward premise expansion, so a literature-wide non-updating ratchet is
not established.


SECTION H: IGNORED CRITIQUE AND NON-UPTAKE
