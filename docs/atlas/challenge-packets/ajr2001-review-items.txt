Prepared 4 November 2026 for atlas.challenge 0.1 (challenge records).

WHAT THIS IS

An external agent (Grok Bot) ran the Provenance citation-network instrument on
Acemoglu, Johnson & Robinson (2001), "The Colonial Origins of Comparative
Development", doi:10.1257/aer.91.5.1369. Atlas imported that run. Its checker
passes the output on form but leaves three items for a person to decide
(PASS-WITH-REVIEW). Atlas records all three as NOT_CHALLENGED.

Your answer to an item becomes one challenge record. That record is attributed to
you, bound to the exact item, and appended next to the audit. It replaces nothing:
the audit, its checker status and its findings stay as they are. You may answer one
item, two or all three. "Cannot determine" is a valid answer.

WHO MAY ANSWER

  - You (the repository owner), recorded as a PERSON.
  - A separately run reviewer, recorded as a MODEL_AGENT.

  Atlas refuses two kinds of answer:
  - from the run's producer (Grok Bot);
  - from the author of this code (the Claude session that wrote atlas.challenge and
    this packet).

  Independence is declared, not verified, so say what the reviewer is and what it saw.

  A reviewer that has already given a view on these items has a prior position, and
  that must be stated. This applies, for example, to the model that wrote the
  "Acemoglu audit v2 - review" note.

ITEM 2  DIRECT_ADJUDICATION_SUBSTANCE
======================================================================
Checker's instruction (verbatim):
  4 source(s) classified DIRECTLY-ADJUDICATING; verify each classification and
  its actual result, favored explanation, weakened explanation, and required
  revision

The four classifications are at 02c lines 196, 197, 445 and 446. Lines 196 and 445
name Sachs 2003 (NBER WP 9490); lines 197 and 446 name Glaeser et al. 2004 (NBER WP
10568). Lines 445-446 are in a second table.

Where to look:
  citation-network/sources/SACHS2003-excerpts.txt    (passage SA-E1)
  citation-network/sources/GLAESER2004-excerpts.txt  (passages GL-E1, GL-E2)
  The full working papers are public (NBER). They are not in the repository.

Questions, for each of Sachs 2003 and Glaeser et al. 2004 (use parts):
  2a. Does the source directly test the rival explanation it is credited with (R1
      direct disease channel; R2 settler human capital)?
  2b. Is its stated result, as quoted or summarized, what the source reports?
  2c. Does the required revision to L3 follow from that result? Compare
      "institutions are a major channel; a direct disease channel is not
      excluded" and "institutions or settler human capital, not separately
      identified".
  2d. Should the four rows count as two classifications or four?

A source that offers a rival explanation has not thereby shown it to be correct.
Say which you are judging.

HOW TO ANSWER
======================================================================

Either reply in plain words, item by item, or fill in a JSON artifact.

Plain words: for each item, give:
  - the outcome;
  - your reasons;
  - the files and lines you relied on;
  - what would change your answer;
  - what you read first;
  - any prior position.
Claude will transcribe your words into the artifact without changing them. It will
record "recorded_by: Claude, transcribing <you> verbatim", and you can check the
file before it is committed.

JSON: print a blank artifact already bound to the exact item. In it:
  - fill every FILL IN;
  - list evidence as {"kind": "FILE", "path": ..., "sha256": ..., "lines": [a, b]}
    or {"kind": "RECORD", "id": ...};
  - add the file to "challenges" in tests/atlas/challenge/ajr2001/context.json with
    its sha256;
  - rebuild.

  python -m atlas.challenge template tests/atlas/challenge/ajr2001/context.json 1
  python -m atlas.challenge build tests/atlas/challenge/ajr2001/context.json \
      --out tests/atlas/challenge/ajr2001/receipt
  python -m atlas.challenge show tests/atlas/challenge/ajr2001/receipt/records.jsonl

The build rejects an artifact if:
  - no reason is given;
  - the outcome is outside the four above;
  - it has a score or verdict field;
  - it cites evidence that does not resolve;
  - it declares the producer or the code author.
An artifact whose target is not the current item is not rejected. It is kept and
shown as detached, and it does not count toward the item.
