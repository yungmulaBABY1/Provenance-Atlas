What this changes for future locked queries
- A relevance-ranked top-N should select relevance_score. Ties and near-ties at
  the cutoff are then visible.
- It should request a few more results than N (for example N+2) and record the
  score margin at the cutoff. A near-tie at rank N would otherwise change
  membership without notice.
- These are recommendations. No instrument has been changed.
