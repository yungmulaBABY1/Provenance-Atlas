PRE-AUDIT CLAIM CONFIRMATION

Field 1 — what was literally measured:
EQ2: attained character or tradeskill level conditional on accumulated character play time in selected active EverQuest II characters, with guild status modeled separately. CR3: logged-in seconds between level-up events conditional on new level in the survey-linked selected sample. Mixed-effects regressions were used.

Field 3 — weaker defensible claim:
In selected samples and fitted models, dichotomously self-reported male status did not confer a consistent advantage on retrieved advancement items. This preserves the significant interactions and does not claim equivalence.

Field 6 — claim under audit:
“Based on our data from two MMOs, there is no significant gender-based performance disparity.”

Two-layer scope discipline:
- Layer A audits Field 6 at the source level: conditional advancement in the two sampled MMOs.
- Layer B audits a downstream mutation: Shen is used to support a general or elite-performance claim about no meaningful sex-linked mechanical difference and a primarily participation/access/retention explanation. Layer B is a MAP GAP relative to Field 6 and cannot by itself anchor the Field-6 verdict.

The citation network can show how authority and wording propagate. It cannot, by itself, prove or disprove the bare real-world proposition that no sex-linked performance difference exists.

SECTION A: CITATION NETWORK STRUCTURE AND CARTEL DETECTION

Corpus definition. The eligible population was all 120 OpenAlex works citing Shen on 2026-08-16. Titles and available abstracts/citation contexts were screened for relevance to gender, gaming, esports, skill/performance, participation mechanisms, or the identified mutation branch. Sixty-five deduplicated works remained; the 50 highest-cited formed the fixed graph corpus. “Internal” means a directed citation from one of those 50 works to another of those 50. This operational definition is reproducible but conditioned on citing Shen and therefore cannot measure the share of the whole field that cites Shen.

Directed-edge result:

- All resolved outbound reference edges from the top 50: 3,236.

- Papers citing at least one other top-50 paper: 26/50 = 52%.
- Papers citing at least three other top-50 papers: 9/50 = 18%.
- Corpus coverage: 50/50 reference arrays present; 3,236/3,236 reported reference identifiers resolved in the retrieved arrays.
- Verification state: GRAPH-VERIFIED for this defined corpus and snapshot.

The Shen anchor was excluded from the closure inference because every eligible paper cites Shen by construction. Additional core dependence was modest: Ratan et al. Conclusion: the retrieved topology does not support a functionally closed citation network. The network is externally referential and diverse at the edge level. A premise can nevertheless be laundered through a sparse network; density and claim transmission are separate questions.

SECTION B: EVIDENCE TYPE CODING

The spine mixes primary observational studies, experiments, neuroimaging studies, qualitative/cultural studies, and reviews. The load-bearing “no disparity” use does not rest exclusively on reviews, but direct evidence is heterogeneous and often measures different constructs: leveling, World of Tanks score, stereotype threat, neural responses, harassment, motives, participation, or addiction. These are not interchangeable tests of elite mechanical performance.

In the top 15, at least ten entries are primary empirical papers and three are reviews; the remaining papers are case/qualitative or mixed-method studies. This diversity is a green flag against a review-only echo chamber. It does not cure the construct mismatch created when the sources are aggregated as if they all directly adjudicate elite performance.

SECTION C: CITATION POLARITY AND CLAIM USE

Semantic Scholar exposed 108 contexts from 55 of 117 indexed citing papers. At least 25/55 context-bearing papers used Shen supportively or as a premise for parity/contextual explanations; this 45.5% figure is a conservative lower bound because fragmentary and background contexts were not forced into polarity categories. No retrieved context explicitly criticized Shen’s measurement, selection, significant interactions, or inference. The remaining papers were background, ambiguous, fragmentary, or used Shen for a nearby subject.

Important supportive uses include:
- Ratan et al. (2020): performance differences are described as attributable to factors confounded with gender, not gender itself.
- Rogstad (2021/2022): perceptions of women as less skilled or unable to compete are described as false, with differences attributed to experience and cultural assumptions.
- Dong et al. papers: Shen is used to establish comparable gaming skills before neural-vulnerability comparisons.
- Later esports papers: Shen becomes evidence that competitive skills are equally attainable or that scientific literature has disproved a performance gap.

Several contexts also repurpose Shen for harassment, toxicity, or exclusion effects that Shen did not directly estimate. This is citation drift, though not every nearby citation is load-bearing.

SECTION D: CLAIM MUTATION TRACKING

The source moves through four distinguishable scopes:
1. Conditional advancement measures in selected characters or linked players.
2. Shen’s own summary: no significant gender-based performance disparity in two MMOs.
3. Hub restatement: performance differences arise from experience/culture rather than gender; perceptions of lower female skill are false.
4. Downstream elite/general restatement: competitive skills are equally attainable, there is no meaningful mechanical gap, or science has disproved a performance gap.

The mutation drops conditioning, sample selection, outcome specificity, the significant EQ2 and CR3 interaction terms, and the level-15 crossover. Rogstad is a major amplification hub, but independent branches through Ratan (2020), Dong and later esports papers mean the mutation is not solely a one-review artifact.

SECTION E: ADJACENT FIELD ENGAGEMENT / NEGLECT

The preregistered question was not “does the literature cite anything adjacent?” It was “does it engage historically available fields capable of changing confidence between the audited and rival mechanisms?” The flip test retained candidates only when auditors favoring either explanation should accept their measurement relevance, adjudicative capacity, methodological independence, and pre-cutoff availability.

Layer A result. Shen substantively engages multilevel modeling and MMO social/guild literature, and it partially engages telemetry and participation research. It does not engage equivalence/noninferiority methods, formal selection/missing-data/transportability methods, or validated active advancement-time measurement. These omissions matter directly to Field 6.

Layer B result. The network engages harassment, sexism, stereotype threat, participation and retention much more often than sports-performance measurement, motor learning, perceptual expertise, deliberate practice, reaction time under load, dynamic tracking, or right-tail selection. In the fixed top-50 corpus, none cited the locked canonical sources for equivalence, missing-data/selection methods, MMO active-time telemetry, deliberate practice, talent development, athlete perceptual expertise, gaming-cognition meta-analysis, dynamic tracking, reaction-time sex differences, multiple-object tracking, or right-tail variability. Three cited Feng et al. on spatial training; four cited Kasumovic and Kuznekoff; sixteen cited Fox and Tang. The asymmetry survives identical citation checking of both explanation families.

This does not prove psychomotor or tail differences explain esports outcomes. It shows that the broader downstream claim has not been adjudicated against the literature most capable of making that explanation gain or lose credibility.

SECTION F: HUB DEPENDENCY AND REVIEW-PAPER LAUNDERING

Rogstad is the clearest premise-laundering hub. It is the fifth-highest-cited paper in the retained spine (126 OpenAlex citations at retrieval), is cited by 5/16 recent retained papers, and restates Shen as evidence against lower female skill and for cultural/experiential explanations. Three of those 16 cite Ratan (2015), four cite Ratan (2020), and eight cite at least one of the three additional core works; five cite Rogstad.

The hub does not dominate the whole graph: only 5/50 retained papers cite it, and recent papers still cite primary sources. Hub risk is therefore “laundering hub,” not proof of a closed network. The risk comes from scope simplification and substitution for direct elite-performance testing, not citation volume alone.

SECTION G: PATTERN RECOGNITION AND RATCHET BEHAVIOR

Proxy-to-construct pattern: logged-in time and level progression are treated downstream as evidence about general gaming skill, mechanical performance, or elite potential. World of Tanks score, stereotype-threat performance, harassment exposure, and neural responses are then combined narratively even though they adjudicate different propositions.

Intervention-evidence ratchet: N/A. The audited explanation is observational and does not make a specific intervention claim whose failed trials could trigger an intervention ratchet.

Model non-updating chain:
- Prediction/model: after conditioning on play time, gender should not produce a performance disparity; apparent gaps are attributed primarily to experience/cultural context.
- Contrary or boundary evidence: Shen reports a significant EQ2 playtime-by-male interaction, a significant CR3 new-level-by-male interaction, and a crossover near level 15; linkage and active-time limitations also bound the inference.
- Reception opportunity: Ratan et al. (2020) and Rogstad (2021/2022) directly cite Shen after publication and therefore had source-level opportunity to encounter those results.
- Model response: both preserve or expand the parity/context model rather than narrowing it to the retrieved outcomes and samples. Later stereotype-threat work does show some updating: Holl et al. (2024) reports two null experiments and calls that branch mixed.

Because the complete chain is verified but overlaps partly with claim mutation and is not uniform across every branch, model non-updating is a mild verified concern (1), not a severe network-wide score.

SECTION J: FALSE-POSITIVE AND BASELINE CHECK

No matched adjacent subfield graph was built, so “unusually high relative to a healthy peer field” is not claimed. The absolute directed-edge share (1.64%), broad external reference base, and mixed evidence types affirmatively rebut a high-density or functional-closure diagnosis under the defined corpus. The Shen anchor’s 50/50 occurrence is a sampling artifact and was never counted as dominance.

The network’s real weaknesses are orthogonal to density: original limitations disappear; different outcomes are treated as commensurate; a review hub expands the inference; no equivalence or matched-exposure elite test is load-bearing; and falsification-relevant fields are thinly engaged. The fair baseline conclusion is “open but selectively adjudicative,” not “cartel.”

ARTIFACT 10: FINAL VERDICT

- Main founding cluster: Ratan et al. (2015), Shen et al. (2016), Ratan et al. (2020), with Rogstad (2021/2022) as the later review hub.
- Main amplification hub: Rogstad’s Gender in eSports research review.
- Main proxy problem: logged-in duration and level progression in selected MMO data become “gaming skill,” mechanical parity, or elite potential.
- Main adjacent fields neglected: Layer A equivalence/noninferiority, selection/missingness/transportability, and active task-time/game-mechanics validation; Layer B sports/perceptual expertise, motor learning, deliberate practice/talent development, dynamic tracking/reaction-time, and right-tail selection.
- Main claim mutation: selected conditional advancement results become a general claim that lower female gaming skill is false and elite/competitive skills are equally attainable.
- Most important ignored critique: none can be scored as ignored; formal critique production was not sufficiently expected, and the specific 2026 public critique is too recent for a reception test. The most important unincorporated boundary evidence is in Shen’s own interaction results.
- Decisive test missing: equivalence within meaningful bounds using advancement-relevant active exposure, activity/assistance controls and selection sensitivity; for Layer B, matched-practice longitudinal mechanical measures and elite-tail outcomes.

- Load-bearing finding(s): missing decisive equivalence/selection tests; proxy transport; multi-branch mutation; and preregistered falsification-relevant adjacent-field neglect. Layer-B findings are MAP GAP and do not independently anchor the Field-6 verdict.
