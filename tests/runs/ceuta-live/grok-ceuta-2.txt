COVERAGE NETWORK & SOURCE-CHAIN AUDIT — MEDIA BRANCH
====================================================
Prosecutor stage. Document-type variant of the citation-network audit.
Runs across MULTIPLE pieces covering the SAME event or claim.

References: shared_primitives.txt §1 (verification states), §2 (finding table)
Companion to: framing_construction_audit_media.txt (single-piece construction)

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS AUDITS AND WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------

The single-piece framing audit asks: is this construction proportionate? That is
a judgment the auditor has to assert.

This audit supplies a BASELINE. Across five outlets covering the same event, a
verb choice stops being the auditor's characterization and becomes an observable
choice: three wrote "drew a knife," one wrote "produced a knife," one wrote
"allegedly had a knife." The variance is the evidence.

Two questions this answers that a single piece cannot:

  1. SOURCE INDEPENDENCE. If all coverage traces to one press release or one wire
     story, "five outlets reported X" is not five confirmations. It is one source
     amplified five times. This is the non-independence problem from the academic
     citation-network audit, with wire copy in place of a shared proxy network.

  2. SHARED CORE vs. CONSTRUCTION. Facts every outlet reports are probably
     established. Material unique to one outlet is either a scoop or a choice.
     The divergence map separates them.

WHAT THIS AUDIT CANNOT DO — STATE THIS IN THE OUTPUT:
It maps how a story propagated and where framings differ. It does NOT establish
which framing is correct. "Twelve outlets framed it this way, three that way" is
a DISTRIBUTION, not a verdict. Majority coverage is not evidence of accuracy —
especially when the majority shares one source. Adjudicating the underlying facts
requires primary material, which is downstream of this audit and human.

COUNTING UNIT — STATE IT BEFORE GIVING ANY NUMBER.

"Independent source" is ambiguous and the ambiguity is not resolvable by
judgment. Three runs on the same event returned 2, 3-4, and ~5 because each
counted a different thing. All three were defensible. None was comparable.

Count at BOTH levels below and report BOTH. Do not report a single integer.

  LEVEL 1 — REPORTING ORGANIZATIONS
  How many distinct organizations produced original reporting — sent a
  journalist, conducted an interview, obtained a document, or made a direct
  observation? An outlet that only republishes or synthesizes does not count
  here, however many pieces it published.
  → 4 (AP, Reuters, AFP via France 24, limited BBC)

  LEVEL 2 — UPSTREAM INFORMATION ORIGINS
  How many distinct origins does the reported material trace back to? Official
  statements from one government count as ONE origin regardless of how many
  ministries spoke. Field observation by one wire team is ONE origin. A
  document release is ONE origin.
  → approximately 5–6 (Spanish official statements [Interior/Ceuta], Moroccan rights groups/observations, migrant interviews, Spanish media [TVE/EFE], Italian/French political statements via X, field visual evidence)

Level 2 will usually be LOWER than Level 1, because multiple organizations
frequently draw on the same official account. That gap is itself the finding:
it measures how much apparent corroboration is really shared dependence.

REQUIRED STATEMENT — use this exact form:

  "This coverage represents 4 organizations producing original reporting and
   approximately 5–6 upstream information origins, across 7 published
   pieces."

AMPLIFICATION RATIO uses LEVEL 2: pieces ÷ upstream origins. State it as
7 ÷ ~5.5 ≈ 1.3.

IF YOU CANNOT SEPARATE THE LEVELS for some material, say so for that material
rather than collapsing both into one number.

Then answer:

  1. How many pieces contain ORIGINAL reporting — a source or document not
     present in the others?
     → ~3–4 (AP both dates, Reuters, partial AFP).
  2. How many are substantially derived from a single upstream source: a press
     release, a wire story, an earlier piece, one official statement?
     → Al Jazeera, NPR, large parts of BBC/France 24 (AP/Reuters copy + official quotes).
  3. INDEPENDENCE COUNTS: report both Level 1 reporting organizations and Level
     2 upstream information origins. Do not collapse them into one number.
     → Level 1 = 4; Level 2 ≈ 5–6.
  4. Does any piece cite another piece in the set? Map the internal edges — this
     is the media equivalent of an internal citation cluster.
     → Multiple pieces cite Reuters/AP estimates and Sbihi quote almost verbatim. Al Jazeera and NPR explicitly credit AP/Reuters.
  5. Where identical or near-identical phrasing appears across outlets, quote it
     side by side. Shared wording is strong evidence of a shared upstream source.
     → “serious humanitarian crisis” / “It’s chaotic” (Sbihi) appears nearly identically in AP, NPR, France 24, Al Jazeera.
     → “Viva España!” / “Long live Spain” shouted by migrants in Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera.
     → Death toll progression: 9 (early AP/Reuters) → 18 (Al Jazeera/NPR/BBC) → 34/57 (later France 24/AP).

If Level 2 is much smaller than the piece count, state the consequence
explicitly: apparent corroboration across outlets is amplification, not
confirmation.
→ Apparent corroboration of numbers and chaos descriptions is largely amplification of AP/Reuters field reporting + Spanish official statements, not independent multi-origin confirmation.

SOURCE-CHAIN STATUS — a SEPARATE axis from verification state
(Do NOT use RECEPTION-CONFIRMED here. In shared_primitives §1 that means a
source's own later reception was checked — replies, replications, corrections.
Comparing an article to a press release is source-chain verification, a different
operation entirely.)

  CHAIN-CONFIRMED ... primary source material or wire copy was available and
                      directly compared against the piece
  CHAIN-INFERRED .... chain inferred from attribution, publication timing, or
                      textual similarity, without the upstream document in hand
  CHAIN-PROVISIONAL . upstream source cannot be established

→ Predominantly CHAIN-INFERRED (textual similarity, attribution, timing). No primary written releases obtained → no CHAIN-CONFIRMED.

VERIFICATION STATE OF THE EVIDENCE USED — media branch subset:

  LOCATED ..................... the item exists; content unverified
  FULL-TEXT-CONFIRMED ......... you read the full source and it supports the claim
  CROSS-OUTLET-CORROBORATED ... the fact appears in 2+ outlets
  CONTESTED ................... outlets report it incompatibly
  PROVISIONAL ................. asserted from memory, or source not retrieved

  RECEPTION-CONFIRMED IS NOT USED IN THIS BRANCH. In shared_primitives §1 it means
  a source's own scholarly reception was checked — replies, replications,
  corrigenda. News coverage does not have reception in that sense. If you reach for
  it, you want CROSS-OUTLET-CORROBORATED or CHAIN-CONFIRMED.

  CROSS-OUTLET-CORROBORATED MUST CARRY THE LEVEL 2 UPSTREAM-ORIGIN COUNT.
  At a low Level 2 count (§B3), multiple outlets reporting a fact is
  AMPLIFICATION of one upstream origin, not corroboration. State the Level 2
  count alongside every use of this label, or mark it PROVISIONAL.

Apply this subset separately to
the documents supporting a finding. The two axes combine — e.g. the police
statement may be FULL-TEXT-CONFIRMED while the outlet's dependence on it is only
CHAIN-INFERRED.

Report both when both apply.
→ FULL-TEXT-CONFIRMED (for the 7 pieces themselves); CROSS-OUTLET-CORROBORATED (facts appearing in 2+ with Level 2 caveat); CONTESTED (death and entry numbers); PROVISIONAL (exact Moroccan official role).

AVAILABILITY MATRIX — REQUIRED BEFORE CALLING ANYTHING "SELECTION"

Outlets publish at different times with different information. Comparing a later
piece (more available) against an earlier brief (less available) and calling the
difference "selection" is the network-branch version of the timing error.

| Fact / source material | Available when Reuters published? | AP 30? | Al Jazeera? | BBC? | France 24? | NPR? | AP 31? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| High 50–60k figures | No | No | No | Partial | Yes | No | Yes |
| Returns of 25k+/48k | No | No | No | Partial | Yes | No | Yes |
| Sbihi quote | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Supreme Court ruling as cause | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

RULE: a fact may be called SELECTION only if it was AVAILABLE before that piece
published and the piece still omitted it. Otherwise it is a TIMING ARTIFACT and
must be labeled so.

State availability basis for each: known (documented release time) / inferred
(other coverage carried it earlier) / unknown. Unknown availability caps the
finding at PROVISIONAL.
→ High numbers and returns only available from ~midday 31 Jul onward → early pieces could not include them (timing artifact, not selection).

THE KEY ASYMMETRY: a fact available to all outlets — present in the primary
source or in earlier coverage — that some include and some omit, where the
pattern correlates with framing. That is selection, and unlike a single-piece
omission finding, it is demonstrable rather than asserted.

NETWORK-WIDE PATTERNS — RECLASSIFY, DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY DOWNGRADE.
If every outlet treats an attribute the same way, that establishes the outlet is not
an OUTLIER. It does NOT establish that the treatment is proportionate or
non-misleading. Every outlet can inherit the same official euphemism, wire framing,
legal convention, or shared blind spot.

  DOWNGRADE: any claim that this outlet is distinctive or slanted relative to peers.
  DO NOT DOWNGRADE: the underlying construction finding itself.
  RECORD SEPARATELY: a network-wide convention finding, which is often the more
  interesting result.

TEST THE SOURCE OF THE UNIFORMITY — these are different findings:
  independent editorial convergence (several newsrooms reached it separately)
  wire inheritance (one wire story, many mastheads)
  common primary source (all worked from one release or briefing)
  legal or professional convention (identification norms, sub judice rules)
  shared blind spot (nobody looked)
Only the first is evidence the convention is considered. The rest are evidence it is
inherited, which leaves the substantive question open.
→ Dominant uniformity on cause attribution and Sbihi quote is primarily wire inheritance + common Spanish official source.

ABSENCE CLUSTER: are the same material attributes OMITTED by outlets that otherwise
share a framing cluster? Shared omission can be more diagnostic than shared wording,
especially where those outlets used DIFFERENT source chains — independent omission is
a stronger signal than common-source omission. Distinguish the two explicitly.
→ Detailed Moroccan perspective and independent verification of numbers largely absent across the sample (common-source omission).

WHAT THE NETWORK ADDS — stated precisely. A single-piece audit CAN establish
causal-context loss by comparing the article against the primary material it
demonstrably used. The network does something narrower and still valuable: it shows
the alternative wording was FEASIBLE AND COMMONLY SELECTED, so the shorter
formulation was not forced by the source.

CAVEAT BUILT IN: outlets repeating the same wire sentence do not independently
demonstrate anything. Before treating cross-outlet agreement as evidence,
establish how many Level 2 upstream information origins it represents
(Section B3).

  1. Which outlets include the causal bridge and which omit it?
     → Most include Supreme Court / trafficking.
  2. Does inclusion correlate with sequencing, sourcing, or framing cluster?
     → Correlates more with availability than lean.
  3. CRITICAL — CHECK THE PRIMARY SOURCE, IN BOTH FORMS. A wording difference may
     be DOCUMENT DEPENDENCE rather than editorial choice: institutions often issue
     a written release AND a spoken briefing with different language, and outlets
     inherit whichever they used. Before rating a verb difference as construction,
     establish which source document each outlet worked from. (Cross-reference
     Section D2 item 2.)
     → Primary forms not obtained → cannot fully separate.
  4. Rank causal-context variance ABOVE synonym variance where it changes the
     reader's model of the sequence.
