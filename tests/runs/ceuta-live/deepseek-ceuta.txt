**STEP 2 — AUDIT YOUR OWN MIRROR**

**Finding against mirror #1 — Severity: MODERATE.** Headline attributes causality to "Open Borders Policy" — an assertion not supported by the shared factual core (the Supreme Court ruling and trafficking misinformation are the cited causes). The mirror converts a complex policy decision into a direct causal claim.

**Finding against mirror #2 — Severity: MODERATE.** "Moroccan authorities watch helplessly" is factually inverted — multiple outlets report Moroccan police used batons, tear gas, and water cannon to push people back. The mirror omits this.
