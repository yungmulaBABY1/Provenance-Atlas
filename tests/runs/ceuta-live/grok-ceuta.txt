=================================================================

1. SHARED CORE (reported by all or nearly all pieces; caveat: low independence count means this may largely reflect the shared source account rather than independent corroboration):
- Large numbers of people (thousands to tens of thousands) crossed from Morocco into Ceuta by swimming around breakwaters / Tarajal and/or scaling or passing fences on 30 July 2026 (extending into 31 July).

- Exact entry numbers: early “2,000–3,000” (Reuters 30 Jul / TVE) → “thousands” → “tens of thousands” / “~49,000–50,000” (Interior Ministry) → “as many as 60,000” (Vivas).

Early lower numbers and lower death counts are largely timing artifacts. Higher later figures became available after many early pieces had already published. 3. CONTRADICTIONS:

No outright contradictory claims on the core sequence of events (mass crossing → military deployment → majority returns).

=================================================================

1. Coverage distribution is not a truth verdict. Majority framing is not evidence of accuracy, particularly at low independence counts.

5. A convenience / partial sample supports findings about the pieces examined, not about coverage generally.
