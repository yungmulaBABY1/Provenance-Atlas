SECTION F — FLIP TEST
=================================================================

PRIMARY DIVERGENCE:
“Breach” versus “cross/enter” changes whether the event is modeled as neutral movement or successful penetration of a defended boundary.

MIRROR HEADLINE:
“After 60,000 people reach Ceuta, Spain militarizes the crossing and sends most back”

MIRROR LEAD:
Tens of thousands of people reached the Spanish enclave of Ceuta from Morocco over two days before Spain deployed troops and arranged the return of most of them. Spanish officials blamed trafficking networks, but migrants described rumors that the border was open and, in one account, Moroccan police directing a group toward it. At least 57 people were reported dead, while officials emphasized restoring control.

MIRROR AUDIT

Finding A:
“Spain militarizes the crossing” converts a reported deployment into a broader claim about policy and intent.
Severity: MODERATE.

Finding B:
“Sends most back” attributes coercive agency to Spain not established by the packet.
Severity: MAJOR.

Finding C:
Placing deaths and complicating migrant accounts before state-control language shifts the piece toward a humanitarian/state-power narrative.
Severity: MINOR.

SYMMETRY CHECK
Type B — symmetric construction risk.

- “Breach” can overstate organized or forceful penetration.
- “Militarizes” can overstate state intent or excess.
- “Returned” can soften coercion.
- “Sent back” can overstate coercion.

AUTOMATIC DOWNGRADE
Candidate claim: security terminology systematically mischaracterizes the event.
Initial severity: MAJOR.
Adjusted severity: MODERATE.
Reason: opposite-direction language can add unsupported implications too.

FORCED PRIOR-DEPENDENCE RANKING
1. Whether “breach” is justified or inflammatory.
2. Whether troop deployment is restoration or militarization.
3. Whether political reactions deserve headline priority.
4. Whether “pour” is vivid description or dehumanizing metaphor.
5. Whether “returned” hides coercion.
6. Whether 50,000 and 60,000 are materially contradictory.
7. Whether death-count escalation is temporal.
8. Explicit wire reuse and source dependence — least prior-dependent.

FLIP-TEST VERDICT
The divergence survives at MODERATE severity. The terms are not interchangeable, but neither directional frame is uniquely immune from overstatement.
