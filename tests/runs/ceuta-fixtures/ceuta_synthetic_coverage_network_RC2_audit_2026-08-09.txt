synonymous formulations repeatedly or convert every vivid verb into a finding.

SCOPE LIMITS
============

1. Coverage distribution is not a truth verdict. Majority framing is not
   evidence of accuracy, particularly where a load-bearing proposition is
   source-dependent, claim-amplified, contested, or origin-incomplete.
2. One event does not establish an outlet's practice. That requires a matched
   corpus across events.
3. Divergence does not establish intent. Observable construction and source
   choice do not prove motive.
4. Wire dependence, length, timing, and deadline explain substantial variance
   here and were tested before any attribution to lean.
5. This convenience fixture supports findings about these eight supplied
   pieces, not coverage generally.
