1. WHAT WE ARE BUILDING — AND WHAT THE WORDS MEAN

Atlas has four design layers:

A. Foundations: the philosophy, evidentiary limits, contestability and human role.
B. Evidence infrastructure: identities, source access, passages, declared scope,
   runs, findings, attributed assessments, reviews, corrections and exports.
C. Investigative methods: versioned checks and evidence gates. Provenance-derived
   research/media methods are supported methods, not the only possible methods.
D. Product surfaces: Research, Media, Browse, reports and eventually APIs/bots.

A prompt instructs a model. A skill packages instructions and tools. An agent uses
tools over multiple steps. A bot is an interaction surface. A runner/executor is
the program or system that performs a declared task. Atlas can use any of these;
none is the product's permanent identity.

Recommended first execution mechanism: a small program with declared inputs and
one bounded model call or short controlled sequence, followed by deterministic
record validation. Keep model output as a new attributed artifact, not an edit to
the source or historical audit. This is a recommendation, not existing code or a
commitment to a provider. Freeze model outputs for mechanical regression tests;
evaluate live model behavior separately.

The durable separation is:
  what the source says;
  what a model or person extracts or infers;
  what a method checks;
  what a reviewer or adjudicator decides.

Reading a passage, matching its text and judging its support are different acts.
The interface must keep that difference visible, even when the workflow is simple.

4. REQUIREMENTS MAP — WHAT MUST SURVIVE THE TRANSITION

This is a family-level traceability map, not an exhaustive line-by-line method
extraction. MAP identifiers below are navigation keys for this document only.
They do not rename native labels, FAIL/GUARD/TOOL codes or existing Atlas IDs.

MAP-01 | Human judgment and contestability
  Preserve attributed judgments, uncertainty, dissent and the ability to challenge
  an assessment. No model vote or composite credibility score becomes authority.
  Origin: S1 reference/philosophy.md, “It points; you judge” and “No composite
  score”; S2 Part II §§1,9; C-03.5–6. Status: partly represented, full workflow open.

MAP-02 | Prior disclosure, symmetry and serious counterevidence
  An operator hypothesis directs inquiry, not its conclusion. Inspect both the
  operator's prior and the field's assumed premises. Seek weakening evidence,
  strongest alternatives and applicable flip/concede tests without criticism quotas.
  Origin: S1 philosophy “Two priors”, “Symmetry”, “An audit must be able to acquit”;
  S2 B-02.1. Status: method requirements; no new executor validation here.

MAP-03 | Stable target and correct attribution layer
  Preserve measured versus claimed, original versus downstream claim, residual
  claim, scope and speaker. Do not blame a source for an inflation introduced later.
  Origin: S1 claim-extraction prompt, philosophy “Trace the claim”; S8 §§2–4.
  Status: native methods exist; structured cross-instrument path not implemented.

MAP-04 | Work, version and accessed bytes are different identities
  Preserve edition-assignment basis, run-local hashes, exact and normalized passage
  hashes with algorithm versions. No silent cross-edition rebasing or semantic merge.
  Origin: S2 A-02.1; S8 §2. Status: bounded identity/anchor implementation exists;
  automatic entity/version resolution and OCR/translation relocation remain open.

MAP-05 | Source reading and eligibility
  Declare required components per check, actual access/extraction and missingness.
  Missing methods block dependent checks; metadata success does not clear a paper.
  Origin: S2 C-03.1, ATLAS-READ-01..07; S1 FAIL-CV-008/[IV-P1].
  Status: bounded offline implementation/tests, not a general PDF-reading service.

MAP-06 | Historical assertion, present verification, assessed support
  Keep all three separate. A current read cannot substantiate an old run's reading
  claim; a valid native label cannot establish physical access or scientific truth.
  Origin: S1 docs/atlas/README.md “Null results”, sources/reviews; S8 I2–I5.
  Status: implemented separation, limited substantive reviews, no human adjudication.

MAP-07 | Method and runner identity
  Pin actual method/criteria, model/runner, declared inputs and versions. Unknown
  identity stays unknown; model-directed criteria get their own provenance.
  Origin: S1 TOOL-004; S2 B-02.1; S8 RunManifest.
  Status: import pins exist; future executor preflight/enforcement still to build.

MAP-08 | Native contracts and historical compatibility
  Preserve instrument-local labels and missing/invalid values. Current grammar
  mismatch is not proof that an old run violated its producing instrument.
  Origin: S1 docs/atlas/contracts and native parser; S2 handoff §4.
  Status: implemented citation profile; historical/multi-instrument parsing deferred.

MAP-09 | Search and criteria timing
  Separate supplied, retrieved, recalled and unavailable evidence; locked query,
  locked sources and post-hoc changes. Target-informed planning is not blind planning.
  Origin: S1 philosophy “Retrieval”; S2 B-02.2, ATLAS-METHOD-06..08.
  Status: imported assertions recorded; external tool-boundary locking not built.

MAP-10 | Source lineage and reviewer exposure
  Count independent origins per relevant fact, not publications or agreeing models.
  Preserve shared sources, prior conclusions seen and independent-to-exposed phases.
  Origin: S1 methodology; S2 Part V, B-02.1/3, C-03.6; S8 I6.
  Status: declarations/review exposure exist; independence is not established by them.

MAP-11 | Append-only identity, challenge and correction
  Same ID/different content is a conflict. Preserve rejected/dead findings, original
  claims and later disagreements. Changed evidence prompts re-review, not auto-verdict.
  Origin: S8 I8; S2 C-03.5, E-01.2; S1 ledger/integrity/reviews.
  Status: ledger and bound reviews exist; general correction/dependency workflow open.

MAP-12 | Exact review target
  Bind a review to the exact finding field/span and evidence descriptors, not only
  a quotation string. Changed offsets/version/component detach the old review.
  Origin: S1 PR #9, atlas/reviews.py, test_evidence_reviews.py.
  Status: implemented/tested atlas-evidence-review/2; /1 retained as retired history.

MAP-13 | Mechanical versus semantic validation
  Separate parser mutations, source correspondence, execution controls and scientific
  judgment. In-vocabulary semantic swaps may require review rather than an invented
  deterministic oracle. Inert text parsing is not proof of agent injection safety.
  Origin: S2 C-02; S1 adapter/anchor/integrity tests. Status: bounded tests implemented.

MAP-14 | Interchangeable evaluation methods
  Support instrument-guided, model-directed and hybrid work under common evidence
  requirements. Native instrument rules govern that instrument's runs only.
  Origin: S2 Appendix B and B-02. Status: governing design; no mode orchestration.

MAP-15 | Empirical validation without test leakage
  Keep tuned regressions, held-out cases, clean controls and unadjudicated ordinary
  papers distinct. Assess misses, false positives, evidence fidelity, abstention,
  stability and cost separately. Model agreement is not an answer key.
  Origin: S2 B-02.3, C-03.3–4; S4 benchmark notes. Status: future validation program.

MAP-16 | Assessment type and remedy
  Separate reproduction, code inspection, robustness, replication, forecasting and
  screening; severity, claim effect, determinability and recommended remedy differ.
  Origin: S2 C-03.2/5. Status: design; no general computational execution instrument.

MAP-17 | Typed graph relationships
  Distinguish source assertions from assessments of support/contradiction/qualification,
  and documented from inferred relationships. No guilt-by-association edges.
  Origin: S2 Part II §§1–3; S8 Claim/ClaimInstance/EvidenceAssessment; E-01.2.
  Status: minimal finding-to-passage mappings exist; rich claim graph not built.

MAP-18 | Portable records and view exports
  Records must survive without the UI. View exports retain scope, filters, as-of
  cutoff, layout/aggregation recipe, denominators, access and review states.
  Origin: S2 A8/A10/E-01; S5 EXP-01..10.
  Status: adapter JSONL/replay exists; full Browse/view-state export is proposed.

MAP-19 | Source-light retention and public release
  Default to audit records, source links, access states and permitted bounded excerpts.
  Public release is separate from local import, with rights/privacy/redaction review.
  Origin: S2 A3, A-02.5–6. Status: local records; public permissions/release not built.

MAP-20 | Honest discovery, counts and metadata refresh
  Distinguish Works, runs, findings and independent origins; disclose corpus selection.
  Preserve provider/version assignments and dated changes; no default person rankings.
  Origin: S2 A-02.3–4, E-01.2; S4 metadata sources.
  Status: proposed catalog/refresh behavior, not a live integration.

MAP-21 | Distinct Media interface and temporal gates
  Event/story -> outlet history -> article/version, using one corpus and as-of scope.
  Separate event, evidence availability, publication, capture and observation clocks.
  Unknown coverage is not “never reported”; missing snapshots are not “no correction”.
  Origin: S2 Media v0.2/D-01, A-02.2. Status: design/reference only.

MAP-22 | Media interpretation and omission limits
  Separate selection/prominence, inherited/chosen material and actor/action/referent/
  stage. Omission requires availability, materiality, opportunity and counterchecks.
  Similarity is not copying; rhetoric change is not motive or a global outlet grade.
  Origin: S1 media prompts, FAIL-MB-017/018; S2 D-01.2–7. Status: native methods exist;
  no Atlas Media runner or validated dashboard. No invented canonical Media Stage 3.

MAP-23 | Future discourse and publishing layers
  Term frequency is not belief, policy adoption or causal effect. A video summarizes
  an existing reviewed audit; it cannot manufacture new findings or product features.
  Origin: S6 pp.6–7; S7. Status: deferred proposals, not universal core requirements.
