Power-Posing Regression Oracle

Status

Regression oracle for reproducibility testing. Not an acquittal or correctness oracle.

This document was derived after the first medium run had already been observed.
Its positive expectations therefore encode that observed result.

A later rerun can test:

• whether the same prompt and packet reproduce the same bounded conclusion;
• whether a prompt change unexpectedly flips the verdict or loses key safeguards;
• whether the execution remains stable across model versions.

It cannot establish that the original substantive interpretation was correct.

Human approval means reading the packet itself and independently agreeing that
the oracle is a reasonable regression target. It does not mean merely approving
or signing off on this document.

Fixture

Use the fixed 12-source, 25-edge supplied power-posing packet from Citation-Network
Compliance Pilot 02.

Do not add sources or perform open-ended retrieval during the scored rerun.

Reproduced high-level result

The bounded network should be classified as healthy or low-risk, not as a
functionally closed, cartel-like, or review-laundered network.

The reason is not that the original power-posing claim survived intact. It did
not. The expected result is that the network visibly updated in response to
failed replications, methodological criticism, preregistration, and later
outcome-specific synthesis.

Reproduced substantive findings

A passing rerun should establish all of the following:

1. Founding-source centrality is real but not dispositive.
P1 should remain the largest or one of the largest bounded citation targets,
but original-source centrality alone must not be treated as closure.
2. The early author review is not load-bearing.
Removing P3 should not reverse the bounded conclusion because direct
replications, critiques, preregistered syntheses, and later meta-analyses
remain available.
3. Critical evidence entered central venues and was engaged.
P2, P4, P5, and P8 should not be classified as suppressed or ignored.
Their findings should be shown to affect later reviews, replies, methods, or
claim scope.
4. The claim narrowed by outcome class.
The strong 2010 package—self-report, behavior, hormones, and actionable
real-world benefit—should not survive intact.
The most defensible surviving claim should concern self-reported felt power or
related self-evaluation, with behavioral and physiological effects weaker,
null, or sensitivity-dependent.
5. Model updating is visible.
Later sources should distinguish:
  • self-report from behavior;
  • behavior from physiology;
  • expansive-versus-neutral from contractive-versus-neutral contrasts;
  • preregistered from non-preregistered evidence;
  • demand-sensitive from less demand-sensitive designs.
6. No field-wide closure claim is warranted from the bounded packet.
The result must remain explicitly bounded to the supplied sources and edges.

Section A regression target

Section A should find a central founding cluster and meaningful internal
connectivity, but should not convert those features into a closure finding
without evidence of blocked dissent, circular dependence, or missing independent
routes.

Expected classification:

• bounded centrality: present;
• reciprocal closed-loop behavior: not established;
• functional closure: not established.

Section F regression target

The hub-removal test should show that P3 amplified the early broad claim but is
not necessary to reconstruct the later mixed, outcome-specific conclusion.

Expected classification:

• early amplification hub: plausible or established;
• review-paper laundering: not established;
• hub dependence: low or non-load-bearing.

Section J regression target

The preregistered-and-critical branch should function as a real comparator and
supply green flags:

• independent direct testing;
• formal methodological reply;
• critique uptake;
• central-journal publication of null and critical results;
• later claim decomposition;
• bias and sensitivity analysis.

Expected classification:

• network behavior compatible with a healthy, self-correcting small literature;
• no suppression or cartel inference.

Verdict regression range

Acceptable labels:

• healthy citation network;
• low-risk citation network;
• open but review-heavy network;
• contested and updating network.

Unacceptable labels absent new evidence:

• cartel;
• functionally closed;
• suppression network;
• review-laundered consensus;
• high-risk citation cartel.

A reasonable bounded cartel-risk score should remain in the low range. The exact
numeric score is not the oracle; the reasoning and classification are.

Concede Test regression target

The following concerns may survive without defeating the acquittal:

• the original paper remains central;
• review and meta-analysis sources outnumber direct primary tests;
• the early author review amplified a broad package;
• some positive syntheses use broad language.

These findings should not mechanically add up to closure.

A passing audit must distinguish:

• “the original finding weakened or failed”
from
• “the citation network was closed or corrupt.”

Interpretation rule

A rerun that matches this oracle has reproduced the prior result. It has not
independently validated that result.

A rerun that departs from the oracle should trigger review, not automatic
rejection. The new run may be wrong, the prompt may have drifted, or the original
run may have been wrong.

The least contamination-prone part of this oracle is the negative failure set
below. Those conditions specify errors the audit should avoid rather than merely
restating the prior output.

Failure conditions

Fail the substantive acquittal fixture if the rerun:

1. infers cartel structure mainly from founding-source centrality;
2. treats failed replication as evidence of network closure;
3. ignores the published and engaged critical branch;
4. claims P3 is load-bearing despite the supplied hub-removal paths;
5. recombines self-report, behavior, and physiology into one surviving effect;
6. makes a field-wide claim from the bounded packet;
7. scores the network high-risk without evidence beyond normal small-field
clustering and review density.

Scoring note

Future reruns may be compared against this document only for reproducibility and
regression detection. They must not be described as passing an acquittal or
correctness test merely because they reproduce the same result.
